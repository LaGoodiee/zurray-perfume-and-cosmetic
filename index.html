Questions people ask before joining

I have never made products before. Is this for me?

Yes. No manufacturing experience is required. We start from the foundations with demonstrations and supervised practice.

Will I take products home?

No. Products made in class are for learning. You take home the skill, the materials and the formulation guides.

What if I live outside Abuja?

You are welcome. You arrange your own travel and accommodation, which is not included. Meals are.

Is the price all I pay?

The fee covers training, scheduled practical ingredients and equipment, materials, breakfast, lunch and 90 days of support.

Will this guarantee me sales or profit?

No one can promise that. We give you the skills, structure and support. Results depend on your effort and market.

Where exactly is the venue?

Public Service Institute of Nigeria (PSIN), Kubwa Expressway, by Dutse Alhaji Junction, Abuja.
